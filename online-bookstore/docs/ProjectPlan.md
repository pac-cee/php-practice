## 1. Introduction & Problem Statement

Modern readers, schools, and authors lack a unified platform to track reading, receive personalized recommendations, test comprehension, and access actionable analytics. BookVerse solves this by offering a centralized system for reading logs, mood-based AI suggestions, quizzes, and robust reporting.

**Key Pain Points Addressed**
- Disconnected tools for tracking, purchasing, and engagement.
- No unified analytics for schools/libraries.
- Limited playful engagement and personalized recommendations.

## 4. Features Overview

**Core Features**
- Centralized reading log & progress tracking
- AI mood-based recommendations
- Integrated quizzes/games for comprehension
- Analytics dashboards (individual & school-admin)
- Exportable reports (CSV/PDF)
- Robust sorting/filtering for analytics

**Security & Compliance**
- Secure authentication (social login, strong passwords, GDPR-ready)
- Data export/delete for users
- Audit logging for admins

**UI/UX**
- Responsive, mobile-first UI

## 5. Technical Stack & Deployment

- **Backend:** PHP (Laravel-style MVC)
- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3 (Grid/Flexbox), ES6+ JS, Blade templates
- **Database:** MySQL
- **Hosting:** Render.com (cloud)
- **Web Server:** Nginx (default)
- **Email:** PHPMailer (mock/test SMTP)
- **Payments:** Stripe & PayPal
