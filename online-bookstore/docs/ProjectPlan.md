## 1. Introduction & Problem Statement

Modern readers, schools, and authors lack a unified platform to track reading, receive personalized recommendations, test comprehension, and access actionable analytics. BookVerse solves this by offering a centralized system for reading logs, mood-based AI suggestions, quizzes, and robust reporting.

**Key Pain Points Addressed**
- Disconnected tools for tracking, purchasing, and engagement.
- No unified analytics for schools/libraries.
- Limited playful engagement and personalized recommendations.
