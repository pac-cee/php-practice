# BookVerse User Guide

Welcome to BookVerse! This guide will help you navigate and get the most out of your reading experience.

## Getting Started
Visit [BookVerse](http://localhost) and create an account or browse as a guest.

## Registering & Logging In
- Register with your email or use Google/Facebook.
- Forgot password? Use the reset link sent to your email.

## Browsing & Searching Books
- Use the catalog to explore books by genre, author, or mood.
- Use filters and sorting for quick access.

## Tracking Your Reading
- Log books you’re reading, set reminders, and track progress.
- Mark books as completed to unlock quizzes and stats.

## Using Recommendations
- Get personalized, mood-based suggestions from the home page.
- Add recommended books to your wishlist or cart.

## Playing Quizzes & Games
- Test your comprehension after finishing a book.
- Play mini-games for fun and learning.
